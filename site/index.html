FilterGram

Instagram, minus the Reels.

A free script for Safari on iPhone. It hides Reels, Explore and suggested posts, and keeps your feed, DMs and the people you follow.

Install on iPhone How it works

What it does

  • Removes the Reels tab, Reels in your home feed, and the Reels tab on profiles.
  • Blocks the endless Reels feed. Visiting it sends you back home.
  • Lets a reel a friend sends you play, but locks it, so you can't swipe to the next one.
  • Hides Explore and suggested posts, so your feed is just people you follow.
  • Every rule has a switch. Tap the small FG button on Instagram to change them.

Install on iPhone

It takes about three minutes. You need Safari (not Chrome) and a free helper app called Userscripts.

  1. Get the Userscripts app Search for “Userscripts” on the App Store. It's free and open source.
  2. Turn it on in Safari Open Settings, then Safari, then Extensions, and switch on Userscripts. Set it to Allow for instagram.com. On newer iOS the path is Settings, Apps, Safari, Extensions.
  3. Pick its folder Open the Userscripts app once and choose a folder in Files where it keeps your scripts.
  4. Add FilterGram In Safari, open filtergram.user.js. Tap the aA menu, then Userscripts, then Install. If you get no prompt, save the file into the folder from step 3.
  5. Open Instagram in Safari and log in Go to instagram.com and log in as normal. Reels and Explore should be gone.

Install the script

Open it from Safari, not from a Home Screen icon. To our knowledge, iOS runs Home Screen web apps in their own window, where Safari extensions don't load, so the filter wouldn't apply. Save instagram.com as a Safari bookmark or Start Page favourite instead.

How it works

FilterGram is a small script that runs inside your own Safari tab, on the real instagram.com. You log in to Instagram directly, as you always do. It changes only what is shown on your screen.

Why a script and not a website you log in to?A site that sits between you and Instagram would have to handle your credentials. That is unsafe, Instagram blocks it, and it breaks their terms.

Good to know

Instagram changes its pages often

FilterGram looks for stable things such as link addresses, not Instagram's scrambled class names, but Instagram can still change things. If Reels sneak back in, update the script: re-open the install link above.

Notifications and posting

The website gets fewer features than the app. Push notifications are limited, and some posting options (like long videos) may need the app. Reading, DMs and photos work fine.

Suggested-post hiding is English only

Hiding suggested posts matches the “Suggested for you” label, so it works when Instagram is set to English. The Reels and Explore rules don't depend on language.
